# Schema notes

Write entries here as you discover them. Do not fill this in from imagination.

When the model gets a field wrong, add the correction here and re-test. That
loop converges fast. A guessed-at glossary documents what you ASSUME it
misunderstands, which is rarely what it actually misunderstands.
